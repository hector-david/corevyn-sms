Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hector Fonseca ("we", "us"), a sole proprietor and the registered brand for the Corevyn text message program, collects, uses and protects information in connection with text messages (SMS) sent from Corevyn, our clinic management software.

Information we collect

  • Your mobile phone number.
  • Your name, when you provide it.
  • The dates and times of appointments we notify you about.
  • The content of text messages you send to us and that we send to you.
  • Your opt-in and opt-out choices, including when and how you made them.
  • Delivery status information returned by mobile carriers.

How we use your information

We use this information only to:

  • send the appointment notifications and replies you agreed to receive;
  • respond to messages you send us;
  • honor your opt-out and opt-in requests; and
  • keep a record of your consent.

How we share your information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party.

We share your phone number and message content only with the messaging provider and mobile carriers that deliver our text messages, only as needed to deliver them, or when required by law.

How we protect your information

Phone numbers and message content are stored encrypted, and access is limited to authorized users.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving text messages. Reply START to resubscribe. Reply HELP for help.

Questions about this policy: hectechsys@gmail.com.
